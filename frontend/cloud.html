Privacy & limits

Data handling

Account credentials are hashed. Saved document chunks, embeddings, conversations and reports are stored in Neon PostgreSQL. AI requests send the consented content to Cloudflare Workers AI. Do not submit confidential, personal or regulated data. AI output may be incorrect; quotations are not a guarantee of accuracy.

Delete individual documents, conversations and workflows from their respective views. Deleting stored content does not necessarily remove prior provider processing or backups. Account deletion and password recovery are not yet available in this beta.

Capacity

Daily limits reset at 00:00 UTC. Daily UTC limits: 12 AI budget units per account, 40 shared across all accounts. Chat and structured answers cost 1 unit; document indexing costs 4; document questions, saved chat turns and report drafts cost 2. Registration and sign-in also have shared daily limits. These are application limits, not provider billing measurements.

Eight documents, eight conversations (ten exchanges each), eight reports per account. Draft approval expires after one hour. The service does not execute external actions.
