Privacy is part of the product.

A plain-English summary of how this local development prototype handles information.

Where your information goes

Chat history, indexed document text and approved reports can be saved in local SQLite databases and files under your KnowledgeAI project folder. Local AI processing is performed by Ollama. This prototype does not need a paid cloud AI API.

Who can access it

The application is intended only for a browser on your own computer. Workspace tokens provide prototype separation, not real account authentication. Data at rest is not guaranteed to be encrypted.

Retention and deletion

Saved content can remain on the computer until deleted. Deleting browser storage or changing a workspace token does not delete database records. Avoid uploading personal, confidential or regulated information during development.

Not ready for public hosting

Production deployment requires real identity and authorization, secure transport, audit controls, robust upload limits, a retention policy and further security testing. Do not expose the development server on a public IP address.
