KnowledgeAI / Safe tools

AI with useful tools.

KnowledgeAI can ask for a calculation or inspect text. The Python server—not the AI model—checks permissions and executes the operation. These tools cannot access files or change your computer.

Small models can sometimes answer without requesting a tool. The result below shows whether a real tool was executed.

Permitted tools

CalculatorReturns the average of 1–20 finite numbers.
Text inspectorCounts words, characters and lines in supplied text.

Safety rules

Only approved, read-only tools. Invalid arguments rejected. At most two calls per question. No file reads, deletion, payments or external actions.
